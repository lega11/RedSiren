About Red Siren

A volunteer-run promoter putting on nights in warehouses and back rooms around the Cork docks.

What we do

We book roughly four nights a month and sell the tickets ourselves rather than through a national agent, which keeps the booking fee at a flat €3 per order instead of a percentage of every ticket. Everyone working the door is a volunteer and any surplus goes back into the next show's guarantee.

What's on

Four kinds of night, all listed on the events page:

Live music
Touring and local bands, usually two supports and a headline.
Club nights
House, breaks and disco until three, one long room.
Festivals
Multi-room all-nighters where one ticket covers every stage.
Spoken word
Seated readings with an open floor at the end.

How to buy

Open an event, choose how many of each ticket type you want, and add them to your basket. You can keep browsing and add more shows — the basket holds them all. At checkout you enter your name and contact details, then pay. You need a free account to complete a booking, which is what lets you find your tickets again later under My tickets.

Student tickets are available on every show at a lower price. Bring a valid student card; the door checks them.
