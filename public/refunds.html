Refund policy

This is a student project. The text below is illustrative and is not legal advice or a binding agreement.

Cancelled events

Full refund including the booking fee, returned to the original card within five working days. We contact everyone on the list by email and phone as soon as a cancellation is decided.

Moved events

Your ticket carries over to the new date automatically. If you cannot make the new date, request a refund within fourteen days of the announcement and you will get the full amount back.

Change of mind

Not refundable. Tickets can be transferred to another person up to 24 hours before doors — email your reference and the new name.
