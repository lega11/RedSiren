Privacy policy

This is a student project. The text below is illustrative and is not legal advice or a binding agreement.

What we store

Your email address, a securely hashed version of your password, and the name and phone number you give at checkout. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and cannot be read back by anyone, including us.

Card details

None are stored. On this project payment is simulated in your browser, and nothing about the card is sent to the server or written to the database.

What we do with it

Contact details are used to confirm bookings and to reach you if an event changes. We do not sell data, and we do not send marketing unless you join the mailing list yourself.
