Frequent questions

The things the door staff get asked most.

How do I buy tickets?

Open an event from the events page, choose how many of each ticket type you want, and add them to your basket. You can add more shows before checking out. At the basket you go to checkout, enter your details, and pay. You need a free account to finish — that is what lets you find the booking again later.

Can I refund a ticket?

Tickets are non-refundable once bought, except where an event is cancelled or moved. If your plans change, tickets can be transferred to someone else — email us with your reference and the new name at least 24 hours before doors.

Where can I find my tickets?

Under My tickets, once you are logged in. Every booking shows its reference, the event, the venue and what you paid. You do not need to print anything.

What payment methods are accepted?

Online we take debit and credit cards. On the door, if a show has not sold out, it is cash only — the card reader is unreliable in the warehouse.

Can I buy multiple tickets?

Yes, up to eight of each ticket type per order. Everyone in your group goes in together on your reference, so arrive at the same time.

What happens if an event is cancelled?

You get the full amount back including the booking fee, to the card you paid with, within five working days. We phone or email everyone on the list as soon as we know, which is why we ask for a contact number at checkout.

What counts as a student ticket?

A valid student card from any institution, checked at the door. If you cannot produce one you will be asked to pay the difference to general admission before going in.

Is the venue accessible?

Marina Warehouse 4 and Docklands Depot are step-free with accessible toilets. The Old Bonded Store has three steps at the entrance and a ramp available on request — tell us in advance and we will have it out and someone on the door expecting you.
